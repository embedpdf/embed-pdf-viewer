The light and dark themes set EmbedPDF's theming variables (`--epdf-accent`, `--epdf-scrollbar-thumb`, `--epdf-toolbar-*`), and the chrome's accent is `--epdf-accent`, with its hover, pressed and tinted shades mixed from it: one variable themes the pages and the chrome. The theme token `accent` sets `--epdf-accent`.
