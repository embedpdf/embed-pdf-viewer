Where a document allows searching but not copying text, search opens as a find bar over the top corner of the pages instead of a panel of empty result rows. It shows which match you're on and how many there are, `Enter` and `Shift`+`Enter` step through them, and its options sit behind a button. In the search panel, `Enter` and `Shift`+`Enter` step through the matches too, and a match the engine finds no text around reads "Result 3" instead of an empty row.
