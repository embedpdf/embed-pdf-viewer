The form layer draws a widget with the painter (SVG text in the engine's own faces) while its look is pending or its picture is of an older version, and the engine's picture otherwise, taken from the form plugin: a typed value, a calculation or a restyle shows at once. The annotation layer applies the same rule, and takes its appearance scale from the render plugin. A text field's editor shows the text being typed where the engine will draw it, in the same face and size.
