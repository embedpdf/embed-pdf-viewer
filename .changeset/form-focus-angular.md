`EpdfForm` has `focus()`, `focusNext()`, `getFocusedField()`, the `focusedField()` signal and `focusChanged$`, and `<epdf-form-layer>` fields take the form's cursor: Tab and Shift+Tab move through the fields in the document's order across pages that aren't on screen, and the arrow keys move between a radio group's buttons.
