'@cloudpdf/protected-display': minor

Screenshot protection: `protectedDisplayPlugin({ player })` shows a document's pages as DRM-protected video for a user who may see them that way (`protected-display.view`) and not render them (no `doc.render`), from the protected display server module. A screenshot, a screen recording or a shared screen shows the page black, while selection, search, annotations and form fields stay on top. It holds the page layout at one page at a time and the zoom at up to 500% (a video is drawn as one surface the size it shows, which older graphics chips can't hold for a page further in), and takes the page pictures over from the render plugin before the document shows. It shows the whole page at about twice its size on screen, and a sharp frame of the part on screen once the view stays still past that; pages turn the way the render plugin's pictures do, without a new frame. The plugin is DOM-free: it picks the DRM system, decides the frames and speaks to the server, and the `player` setting plays them, `browserProtectedPlayer()` from `@cloudpdf/web` in a browser, best as a function that imports it so it loads only for a protected user. Without a player it stays off. `getStatus()` says whether the pages show as protected video, and why not.
