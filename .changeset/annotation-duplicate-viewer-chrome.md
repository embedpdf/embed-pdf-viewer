New command `annotation:duplicate`, with `Ctrl`/`Cmd`+`D` and a button in the bar over a selection. It copies the selected annotations beside the originals and selects the copies, as one step to undo. The button shows for annotations people move, so not for a highlight, and is greyed out while a locked annotation is selected. New icon `duplicate` and label `commands.annotate.duplicate`, in all 15 languages.
