Duplicate (`annotation:duplicate`, `Ctrl`/`Cmd`+`D`) now copies form fields in Form design: a radio button's copy joins its group as a new choice, and any other field's copy is a new field named after the original. The form design sidebar has **Duplicate group** for a radio group, which copies the whole group as a new one. New label `formDesign.duplicateGroup`, in all 15 languages.
