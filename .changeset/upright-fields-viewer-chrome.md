Turn form fields in the form designer. A selected field's menu has two new commands, `annotation:rotate-clockwise` and `annotation:rotate-counterclockwise`, which turn it a quarter turn, and the Style sidebar has an Orientation control, which turns what's in the field and leaves its box where it stands. A field placed on a page that is shown turned reads upright there. The new labels are translated in all fifteen languages.
