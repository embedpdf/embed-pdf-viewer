Turn form fields in the form designer. Two new commands, `annotation:rotate-clockwise` and `annotation:rotate-counterclockwise`, turn the selected fields a quarter turn. A selected field's menu shows the clockwise one, and the other is there for your own menu or shortcut. The Style sidebar has an Orientation control, which turns what's in the field and leaves its box where it stands. A field placed on a page that is shown turned reads upright there. The new labels are translated in all fifteen languages.
