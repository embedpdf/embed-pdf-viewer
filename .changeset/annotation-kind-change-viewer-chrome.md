The style panel has a **Type** row for a selected highlight, underline, squiggly line or strikethrough, and for a square or circle: one button per kind it can become, each with its tool's icon. Picking one changes the annotation's kind in place, with its comment and replies. The `annotation.style.kind` label is translated into every viewer language.
