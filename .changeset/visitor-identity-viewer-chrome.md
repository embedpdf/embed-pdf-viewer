The viewer asks for what a share link waits for: its passphrase, or who the visitor is, in the document's tab before it opens, and in a dialog when a change waits for the visitor's answers. The questions show the link owner's purpose and privacy notice, say that the visitor's name shows on what they add, and that the answers can't be changed later. A tab waiting for a passphrase or answers shows a lock. Translated in every language the viewer ships.
