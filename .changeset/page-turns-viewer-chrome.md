The View menu's Scrolling group gains Page by page (`view:scroll-paged`, icon `pageByPage`), next to Vertical and Horizontal: one page or spread at a time, turned by scrolling or swiping past its edge. Vertical and Horizontal now also switch back to scrolling every page, and a lock on `flow` or `layout` greys them out. The label is in all 15 languages.
