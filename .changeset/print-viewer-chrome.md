**Print** (`document:print`, `Ctrl`/`Cmd`+`P`) opens a Print dialog: every page, the current page or pages such as `1-3, 5`, and whether annotations print. Nothing is made while the user chooses: **Print** prepares the chosen pages, shows how far it is with a progress bar (Cancel stops it), and opens the browser's print dialog when they're ready. In Safari, which prints only from a tap, it says the pages are ready and prints from the next tap on **Print**. It says when the document allows only low-resolution printing. In every language the viewer ships.
