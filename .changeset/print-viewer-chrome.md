**Print** (`document:print`, `Ctrl`/`Cmd`+`P`) opens a Print dialog: every page, the current page or pages such as `1-3, 5`, and whether annotations print. It prepares the pages as it opens and again when a choice changes, shows how far it is, and says when the document allows only low-resolution printing; its **Print** button opens the browser's print dialog at once. In every language the viewer ships.
