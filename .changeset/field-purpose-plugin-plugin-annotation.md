A lender can give a record a placeholder with `placeholderOf()`: the words an empty form field shows for its purpose, which the annotation layer writes in the field's box in design mode.

A lender can also give a record an outline with `outlineOf()`, and paint what its tools place with `toolPaintOf()`: a form field tool's ghost is then the field it places, see-through, with its look, tint, outline and placeholder.
