Share links can ask their visitors who they are. A link's `visitorIdentity` names the fields it asks (`displayName`, `email`, `title`, `organization`, `organizationalUnit`, each required or optional), when it asks (`'on-open'` or `'before-first-change'`), and the owner's `purpose` and `privacyUrl`. The exchange takes the answers as `visitorIdentity`; until the required ones are given, a link that asks on open answers 422 `VisitorIdentityRequired`, and one that asks before the first change issues the session with `visitor.identityRequest` and refuses its writes with 403 `VisitorIdentityRequired`. Answers are stored once per visitor (migration 042, `share_visitors`), with the time they were given, and go into every token issued for them as its `identity`; an answer once given is never replaced, and a visitor who starts over is a new visitor who is asked again. `GET /v1/tenants/{tenantId}/shares/{shareId}/visitors` lists a link's visitors (who answered or made a change) newest first with their layer, and `DELETE …/visitors/{visitorId}` erases a visitor's answers and layer, on archived links too. A link with visitors is archived rather than deleted (409). The idle clock is now the visitor's: `visitorIdleSeconds` (renamed from `visitorLayerIdleSeconds`) deletes the visitor and their layer once unseen that long, only on links that set it.
