The viewer speaks Swedish (`sv`) and Traditional Chinese (`zh-TW`), for fifteen languages. Readers of Traditional Chinese (`zh-TW`, `zh-HK`, `zh-MO`, `zh-Hant`) now see it instead of English. Both are machine-translated in the terms Adobe Acrobat uses in each language, are waiting for a native speaker's review, and load the first time the viewer shows them. `strings` an app gives for `sv` or `zh-TW` now change the built-in language's words, and the words it leaves out show in that language instead of English.
