Comment cards show what their thread has and offer what comes next. A mark with nothing said yet shows one box to write the comment, which becomes the annotation's own text where it may change, and a reply where it may not. A thread shows its comment, its replies and a box to reply; the box shows on the selected card, and stays while it holds a draft. A thread's status shows as a chip by its date once one is set. A check on the card marks the thread completed, and the card's menu (its ⋯ button, or a right-click on the card) sets any review status and deletes the thread. A page's count says how many comments are written on it. New commands `comment:status-accepted`, `comment:status-rejected`, `comment:status-cancelled`, `comment:status-completed`, `comment:status-none`, `comment:delete-thread` and the `comment-item` menu, and the icons `circleCheck` and `send`.
