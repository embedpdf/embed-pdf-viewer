Follows the documents, interaction and stage contracts: `useDocuments` exposes `getActiveId`, `retry`, `rename`, `save`/`saveLayer`; new `useDocumentEvent`. Stage hooks read through `getSettings` and the page registry (`usePages().pageCount`, `usePageList().pages` come from `documents.listPages`); `useScrollMetrics`, `<Scrollbar>` and `<Stage>` bind the host lens. The actions adapter navigates with `goToPageIndex`. `SelectionLayer`, `SelectionMenu` and `SelectionHandles` follow the selection contract (`listSegments`, `getAnchor`, `isGestureActive`, `ensureLoaded`); the annotation-side anchor type is `AnnotationSelectionAnchor`. `RenderLayer` binds the render host lens: `renderSource`/`getSourceKey` for the base plane and a `ViewDemand` (`setDemand` in a layout effect, pure `getPlan`) for the tile plane. The annotation surface follows the annotation contract: `useAnnotationSelection` returns refs, `useAnnotationStatus` (was `useCommentsHydration`) returns a `ResourceStatus`, `useFilePickerProvider` uses the provider's remover, the menu anchors through `getSelectionAnchor`, and the text editor drafts through the host lens. The form surface follows the form contract: fill items address fields by `fieldRef`, `useFormField` resolves through `getFieldForWidget`, the fill layer binds the host lens (re-exported as `FormHostToken`). `LinkLayer` follows the link contract (`listLinks`, `ensureLoaded`, `getLabel`, the host `isNavigationEngaged`). The stamp hooks follow the stamp contract (`listLibraries`, `listAssets`, `getAssetPreview`). `useViews` is `usePanes` (`panes`, `focusedPaneId`, pane and tab verbs per the view-manager contract); `useSurface` and `useMenus` read through `getSurface` and `listOpenMenus`. Phase 4 (cross-cutting): `StageScope` and token-free stage hooks and chrome (`<Stage>` scopes its own pages and overlay), `useStageEvent`; the controlled `<Stage tool onToolChange>`, `useTool` gains `push`/`pop`, `useToolChanged`; a `use<Plugin>Event(pick, handler)` per plugin with events, plus `useInteraction`, `useRender`, `useActions`, `useViewManager`; `useSelectionState`, `useAnnotationList`, `useFormValue`, `useCalibrationRequest`; `useRedaction().lastResult` (was `lastApplyResult`) and `usePendingRedactions(filter?)`; `Viewer` forwards a kernel `ref` and takes `onReady`; development-only warnings for a `RenderLayer` that still bakes annotations under an `AnnotationLayer`, a `FormLayer` beside `formWidgetRenderer`, an inline `renderers` array, a second `useFilePickerProvider`, and `SelectionHandles` outside a stage; `<PageView>` takes `pageRef` or `pageIndex` (was `page`) and a `fallback`; `<SearchLayer onHitClick>`; the root barrel re-exports the form, search, i18n and actions entries in full.
