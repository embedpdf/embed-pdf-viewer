Add the viewer's layout: bars with a `place` (an edge with `before`/`after`, `{ float }`, `{ with }`, `{ at: 'selection' | 'annotation' }`), a `mode` and a `title`, menus with titled sections, panels and `tabs`, all as plain data. `createLayout(data)` makes the immutable layout whose chainable edits (`place`, `addBar`, `removeBar`, `add`, `remove`, `replace`, `addMenu`, `addPanel`, `panel`, `removePanel`, `tabs`) each return a new one, `toJSON()` gives the data back, and `resolveLayout(option, defaults, env)` runs a `(layout, env) => layout` function. `compileBar` turns a layout bar into the `BarSchema` a toolbar fits, with the mode tabs derived from the bars' modes; `validateLayout` names every unknown id with the closest one that exists. A custom item without a command never leaves its bar; its normalized `terminal` is `null`, and `commandOfUnit(unit)` gives the command a unit runs, or `null` for such an item. `ChromeSchema`, `FrameSchema`, `MenuSchema`, `chromeHelpers`, `defineChrome`, `validateChrome`, `addItem`, `removeItems` and `replaceItem` are removed.

The toolbar fit runs along either axis: it counts lengths, widths for a horizontal bar and heights for a vertical one. `solve(bar, metrics, containerLength)`, `FitResult.length` and `ToolbarLayoutInput.containerLength` replace their `width` names, and a custom item's `measure(length)` reports its length along the bar. `layoutToolbar({ orientation: 'vertical' })` draws and measures every command of a vertical bar at `'icon'` only, with no smaller step; custom items keep their own variants.
