Take a Snapshot, in the document menu: drag a box over a page, or click a page for all of it, and a bar under the box copies the picture (`Ctrl`/`Cmd`+`C`) or saves it as a PNG; `Escape` lets it go. New commands `tool:snapshot`, `snapshot:copy`, `snapshot:save` and `snapshot:clear`, the `snapshot` bar, and the `snapshots` feature for `disable`. A PDF that forbids copying has no Take a Snapshot. In all fifteen languages.
