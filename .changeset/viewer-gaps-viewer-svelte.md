`Button` and `MenuItem` take `active`: shown pressed, or checked, as a command's button or row is while its command is active, for a toggle of your own (a theme switch). `Icon` draws one of the viewer's icons, or one of yours (`icons`), in the color of the text around it: `name`, `size` (default 20) and `label` for screen readers.
