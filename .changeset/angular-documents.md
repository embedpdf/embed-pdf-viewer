'@embedpdf/angular': minor
---

Add the Angular services for working with documents. `@embedpdf/angular/view-manager` has `withViewManager()` and `EpdfViewManager`, with `panes()` and `focusedPaneId()` as signals, the calls that split, close and move panes and their tabs, and `paneCreated$`, `paneRemoved$`, `focusChanged$` and `documentMoved$`. `@embedpdf/angular/page-edit` has `withPageEdit()` and `EpdfPageEdit`, to rotate, move, insert, delete and extract pages. `@embedpdf/angular/metadata` has `withMetadata()` and `EpdfMetadata`: the standard fields as `fields()` and their load state as `status()`, `update()`, `refresh()`, `canUpdate()`, `updated$` and `resynced$`, and your own fields as the `custom` namespace (`custom.fields()`, `custom.status()`, `custom.update()`, `custom.refresh()`, `custom.updated$`), which is never itself a signal. `@embedpdf/angular/actions` is now `withActions(config)`, `EpdfActions` (the calls, the checks, the settings and the streams such as `diagnosticReported$`) and `withActionsUi(handlers)`, which installs the browser's defaults for websites, printing and alerts on the active document's actions, in the same change that makes it ready; `handlers` replace any of them, and can be a function that runs once in an injection context, so they can use your services. `injectActionsUiAdapter()` is gone. Each entry re-exports its plugin, so registration travels with the UI.

Every plugin service now has `getSettings()` next to `settings()`, working with or without a document. A check (`canUpdate()`, `canCopy()`, `isScriptingEnabled()`) read in a template, a `computed()` or an `effect()` is read again after each change of the viewer and wakes its reader only when its answer changes, so a button it disables follows the document, the selection and the permissions. A namespace's calls are built with `CapabilityBinding.namespaceMethod()`.
