`@embedpdf/angular/history`: undo and redo, `withHistory()` and `inject(EpdfHistory)` (`undo()`, `redo()`, the signals `canUndo()`, `canRedo()`, `undoLabel()`, `redoLabel()`, and `undone$`, `undoFailed$`).

An annotation or a field that shows again before the page's next pictures arrive, such as one an undo brings back, shows its picture at once.
