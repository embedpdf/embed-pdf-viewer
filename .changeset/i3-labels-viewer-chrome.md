The style panel speaks the viewer's language: its property labels, the line endings, blend modes, fonts, alignments and border styles, the flags and what each does, a form field's settings, the link editor, the thumbnails' tooltips and the panel's own words read from the language packs, by the libraries' `labelKey`s (`annotation.style.*`, `annotation.flags.*`, `form.settings.*`). Every key is in all 13 packs; the new words are machine-written and marked for a native speaker's review, as the other packs' are. The line-ending and border pickers name their options for screen readers. Readers of Traditional Chinese see English rather than Simplified Chinese, and the Go to Page dialog reads "Page [3] of 8" again (it split the sentence at every character).
