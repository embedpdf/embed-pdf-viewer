'@embedpdf/svelte': minor

Add the Svelte bindings for viewing and text. `@embedpdf/svelte/page-view` has `<PageView>`, one page on its own with no Stage: give it a page (its `ref` or its index) and a `width`, and write its layers between the tags (`{#snippet children(page)}` when they need the page), with `fallback` and `pageChrome` snippets and `class` and `style` on the outer box; a menu anchored inside it is placed on the body, where no card clips it. `@embedpdf/svelte/selection` has `<SelectionLayer>`, `<SelectionMenu>` (shown over a settled selection), `<SelectionHandles>` (the touch handles, in a Stage's overlay), `<SelectionClipboard>`, `copySelection()` and `useSelection()`, `useSelectionState()`, `useSelectionSettings()`, `useSelectionEvent()`. `@embedpdf/svelte/search` has `<SearchLayer>` (clickable matches with `onHitClick`), `useSearch()`, `useSearchState()`, `useSearchSettings()`, `useSearchEvent()` and `useSearchHits(page?)`, every match or one page's as `{ current }` (pass the page as a function to follow a prop). `@embedpdf/svelte/link` has `<LinkLayer>`, a real anchor over each link that you can draw around with its `link` snippet (`{@render native()}`), `useLink()`, which opens websites while it's in use, and `useLinkEvent()`. A press on a link or a selection handle never reaches the Stage. Each entry re-exports its plugin, so registration travels with the UI.

A `<PageView>` shows the document its `<DocumentScope>` names (else the active one), and with the interaction plugin registered it is the page's pointer surface by itself, below its layers.
