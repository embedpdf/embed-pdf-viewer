The form designer. The Form mode opens for filling in; its **Form design** button (`form:design`) switches design on: fields select, move, resize and delete, and the form design sidebar (`form-design`) opens. A field tool switches design on too, and pressing Form design again or leaving the Form mode ends it. The sidebar lists the document's fields by page, and a click selects one and scrolls to it; with a field selected it shows the field's settings and, for a radio group or a checkbox, its buttons with **Add a button** or **Add a box**, which adds one next in line and selects it. A field's look stays in the Style sidebar, now on the Form toolbar too, and its settings left it. Every word is in all 13 languages.
