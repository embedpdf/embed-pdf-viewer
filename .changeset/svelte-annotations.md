Add the Svelte bindings for annotations, `@embedpdf/svelte/annotation`. `<AnnotationLayer>` draws a page's annotations, the selection's outline and handles, the tool's preview and the text boxes being typed in; `renderers` draw some annotations with your own component (it gets the annotation, the `frame` it draws into and the layer's own drawing as a `native` snippet, `{@render native()}`; an `interactive` renderer takes the pointer, so its buttons can be clicked), and the `handle` and `rotationHandle` snippets draw the handles your way. `<AnnotationMenu>` floats your menu next to the selection, `<AnnotationDraftMenu>` next to a shape being drawn click by click (its `children` snippet gets the shape), and `<AnnotationRotationBadge>` follows a turn in progress. The readers are `useAnnotation()`, `useAnnotationState()` (a reactive object: `status`, `selected`, `hovered`, `editing`), `useAnnotationSettings()` and `useAnnotationEvent()`; `useAnnotationList(filter?)`, `useAnnotationDefaults(toolId)`, `useAnnotationProperties(toolId?)`, `useAnnotationAnchor(ref)`, `useCommentThreads()` and `useCommentThread(ref)` are `{ current }` (pass an argument as a function to follow a prop), and `useComments()` is the comments API. `useRichTextEditor(annotation)` makes your own element a text box's editor: put `{@attach editor.attach}` and `style={editor.style}` on it, and read `editor.editing`. `useFilePickerProvider(provider?)` gives click-then-pick tools (a stamp from a file, a file attachment) the browser's file dialog, or your own picker, while the component lives. The entry re-exports the annotation plugin, so registration travels with the UI.
