A callout is its own kind, `free-text-callout`: `kindOf` returns it for a free text with the callout intent, and render items, behaviors and custom renderers see that name. A callout keeps the turn it was made with, the one that stands it upright on a turned page, so a selected callout shows no rotate knob, rotate 90° and reset rotation leave it as it is, and a selection that includes a callout can't turn or resize as a group. Reset rotation now skips every kind that doesn't turn, so a caret keeps the turn of its text.
