Who fills a form field in, as one `assignment`: a create (`POST .../form/fields`), an update (`PATCH .../form/fields/{field}`) and the `forms.create` and `forms.update` ops of a change take it beside `groupId`, and every field a response carries has it. Every token that may fill in some field fills in a field anyone fills in; giving a field to anyone takes `fields:set-group:all`. A signature without a lock of its own locks every such field that holds a value.

The form's and the form export's engine values are raised, so answers with the new member get new URLs.
