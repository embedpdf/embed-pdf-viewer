The stamp contract, on the kernel's `create()` hook. Reads: `listLibraries(filter?)` (was `libraries`), `getLibrary`, `listAssets({ libraryId?, kind?, category? })` (was `assets(libraryId?)`), `getAsset`, `getAssetPreview` (was `assetPreview`), new `renderAssetPreview(id, { width })`, `readAssetBytes` (was `assetBytes`; returns a copy). Libraries: `deleteLibrary` (was `removeLibrary`), `importLibrary` (was `importLibraryPdf`), `exportLibrary` is async and rejects `not-found`. Assets: `createAsset` (was `addAsset`), `createAssetFromAnnotations` (was `addAssetFromAnnotations`), `deleteAsset` (was `removeAsset`), new `moveAsset` (copy to another library, then delete) and `duplicateAsset`. Placement: `getArmedAsset` (was `armedAsset`; pure), new `placeAssetOnPages` (a `BatchResult<AnnotationRef, PageRef>`), `canPlace`, `canImport`. Events: `onLibraryCreated`/`onLibraryUpdated`/`onLibraryDeleted`, `onAssetCreated`/`onAssetUpdated`/`onAssetDeleted`, `onArmChanged`, and `onLibraryChanged` (bytes changed — what persistence subscribes to). The PDF-convention helpers (`stampKey`, `stampPieceInfo`, the PieceInfo names, …) moved to `/internal`; `DEFAULT_LIBRARY_KIND` stays public. Refusals are `PluginError`s.
