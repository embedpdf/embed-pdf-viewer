A `fonts` entry, as React's: the fonts plugin, `useFonts()`, `useFontsState()`, `useFontsEvent()` and `useFontMounts(programs)`. A free text box mounts the programs its text is drawn in and draws with `font-synthesis: none`, a text field's editor is laid out again once its fonts are found, and a list box draws its rows in the widget's font. `mountWebFont` is no longer exported from `/runtime`.
