The form contract, on the kernel's `create()` hook. Every method takes a `FormFieldRef` (`fieldRef.byName` / `fieldRef.byObjectNumber`) or a widget's `AnnotationRef`; the `obj:`/`fqn:` field keys leave the public surface. Reads: `getSnapshot` (was `snapshot`), new `getStatus`, `listFields(filter?)`, `getField`, `getFieldForWidget`, `getValue`, `listWidgets` (the fill items, now carrying `fieldRef` and `annotationRef`), `getWidgetAt`. Filling: `setValue` is the validated path (was `commitValue`) resolving a `SetValueResult`; `setValueRaw` is the engine passthrough (was `setValue`); `setText`, `setChecked` (was `toggle`), `setChoice` (was `choose`) resolve with the result; new `setValues`, `resetAll`, `exportValues`, `importValues`; `activateWidget(widget)` takes the widget alone. Designing: `createField({ family, page, bounds, name?, appearance?, options? })` (was `placeField`), `attachWidget`, `detachWidget(ref, widget)`. Events: `onValueChanged`, `onFieldCreated`/`onFieldUpdated`/`onFieldDeleted` (from the confirmed document events, own and remote), `onValidationRejected`, `onResynced`. `FormConfig.validation: 'scripts' | 'none'` replaces the empty options. Refusals are `PluginError`s (`permission-denied`, `not-found`, `invalid-input`, `not-ready`). A refresh that is asked for while a read is in flight now queues one follow-up read (G5).

Host (`/contract/host`): `listFillItems`, `getFillItem`, `ensureLoaded` (was `ensureGeom`), `getPageBox`, `notifyWidgetEvent(fieldRef, widget, event)`, `resetFormAction`, `resolveSubmitDataset`, `commitScriptFormEffects`.

- Internal layout: the plugin is `contract` / `host-contract` / `model` / `controller` with `services`, `read`, `write`, `sync`, `scripting` and `tools` areas; the `./scripting` export now resolves to `scripting/controller.ts`.
