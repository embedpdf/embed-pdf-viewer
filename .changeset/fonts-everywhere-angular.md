A `fonts` entry: `withFonts()` and the `EpdfFonts` service (the list and its status as signals), and `injectFontMounts(programs)` in `/runtime`. A free text box mounts the programs its text is drawn in, a text field's editor is laid out again once its fonts are found, and a list box draws its rows in the widget's font. `mountWebFont` is no longer exported from `/runtime`.
