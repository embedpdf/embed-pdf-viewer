The style panel, its flags, a form field's settings, the link editor and the thumbnails' tooltips are translated in all 13 languages, by keys an app can change in `strings` (`'annotation.style.stroke'`, `'form.settings.tooltip'`). Readers of Traditional Chinese see English rather than Simplified Chinese. The Go to Page dialog reads "Page [3] of 8" again.
