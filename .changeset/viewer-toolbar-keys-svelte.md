`<Toolbar orientation="vertical">` stands a toolbar on its side: it measures heights and fits the height it's given, stacks its sections top, middle and bottom, and draws every command at `'icon'`, with the default menus opening beside it. The toolbar is now a `role="toolbar"` with `aria-orientation` and an optional `aria-label` prop, and one tab stop: the arrow keys along it move between its items (the first focusable element of your own item is its stop), past disabled ones, with Home and End to the ends and Left and Right swapped in right-to-left text. The default "More", folded-group and group-trigger menus open on their first row and take the arrow keys, typeahead, Enter, and Escape, which closes the menu and puts focus back on its button; the toolbar drawing again while one is open leaves focus where it is. The measuring copies are `inert`. `separatorWidth` is the separator's thickness across the toolbar, and the `custom` snippet's `ctx.measure` takes a length: the item's height in a vertical toolbar.
