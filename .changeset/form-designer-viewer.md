The form designer: the Form mode opens for filling in, and **Form design** (`form:design`) switches design on, with a sidebar that lists the fields by page, shows the selected field's settings, and adds buttons to a radio group or boxes to a checkbox next in line. A field's look is in the Style sidebar, on the Form toolbar too. Selecting a field in the Form mode works again: the form plugin no longer misses the annotation plugin.
