# Put a PDF on your website

In five minutes, this is on your page: your PDF, in a viewer people can scroll, zoom, search and
read on any device. You need a CloudPDF account and a page you can edit. No server, and no code of
your own.

<GrantPreview grant="shr_xs-9Y5qwuYrJ2BKtWzuo_2xy" />

## 1. Upload a PDF

Sign in to the [dashboard](https://app.cloudpdf.com), and on **Documents**, click **Upload** or drop
the file on the page.

<Screenshot
  name="documents"
  alt="The Documents page of the Fernquill Studio workspace, with its folders and PDFs, and the Upload button"
/>

CloudPDF keeps the file and gets its pages ready to show. A large PDF takes a few seconds.

## 2. Make a grant

A grant lets people open the document without an account. Open the document, then click **New
grant**:

<Screenshot
  name="new-grant"
  alt="The page of Brand guidelines.pdf: its size and ID, and a Grants card with the New grant button"
/>

Give the grant a name you'll recognize, such as "Website". The rest is already right for a public
page: people can **Read** the document, and it opens **Anywhere**.

<Screenshot
  name="grant-dialog"
  alt="The New grant dialog: named Website, people can Read, and it opens Anywhere"
/>

Click **Create grant**.

## 3. Paste it into your page

Choose **Embed on a website**, and copy the code:

<Screenshot name="grant-embed" alt="The Grant created dialog, with the embed code for HTML" />

It's two lines. Put them where the PDF should appear:

```html
<script type="module" src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@cloudpdf/viewer@3/dist/cloudpdf.js"></script>

<embedpdf-viewer grant="grt_4n0hW2ubtHdYCRnkwu8My8Pb" style="height: 640px"></embedpdf-viewer>
```

The viewer fills the box you give it, so give it a height. Open your page, and the PDF is there.

## 4. Or send the link

A grant that opens Anywhere has a link of its own. Send it in an email or a chat, and the document
opens full screen:

<Screenshot name="grant-link" alt="The Grant created dialog, with the grant's link to copy" />

## Change it whenever you like

A grant keeps working until you change it. From its menu on the document's page, you can:

- let people do more: **Comment**, **Download** or **Print**;
- ask for a passphrase, or let the grant expire on a date;
- pause it, and resume it;
- end it, which stops its link and every page that embeds it, at once.

Every page that embeds the grant follows on its next visit, so you never touch your code again.
