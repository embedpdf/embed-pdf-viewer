# Plans and usage

A workspace runs on a plan. Both plans have every feature: the viewer, grants, tokens, API keys,
and as many members as you like. They differ in how much the workspace uses each month. Prices
are on [Pricing](/pricing).

| | Standard | Growth |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| PDF views a month | 10,000 | 50,000 |
| PDF uploads a month | 10,000 | 50,000 |
| Storage | 10 GB | 50 GB |

A **view** is someone opening a document: a visit through a grant, or a token used for the first
time. Turning pages, searching and coming back to the tab don't count again.

## Your trial

Your first workspace starts with 14 days free on either plan. Each account gets one trial, so a
second workspace pays from its first day.

## What this month used

**Usage** shows the views, uploads and storage of the current month, against your plan:

<Screenshot
  name="usage"
  alt="The Usage page: documents uploaded, document views and storage this month, each against the plan"
/>

The bars turn orange at 80% and red at 100%. Going over doesn't stop anything: your documents keep
opening, and owners hear from us about a bigger plan.

## Change your plan

Owners and admins change the plan, the card and the billing details on **Billing**, where the
invoices are too.

Without a plan, the workspace keeps your documents, but they don't open: links and embeds stop until
it has a plan again. Members, Billing and Settings still work.
