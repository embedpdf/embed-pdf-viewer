# Plans and usage

A workspace runs on a plan. Both plans have the same features: the viewer, grants, tokens, API keys
and members. They differ in how much the workspace uses each month. Prices are on
[Pricing](/pricing).

| | Standard | Growth |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| PDF views a month | 10,000 | 50,000 |
| PDF uploads a month | 10,000 | 50,000 |
| Storage | 10 GB | 50 GB |

A **view** is someone opening a document: a visit through a grant, or each time a viewer opens it
with a token. Turning pages and searching don't count again, and neither does a grant visit that
stays open.

Screenshot protection is an add-on, not part of a plan. **Billing** lists it: contact us to add it.

## Your trial

Your first workspace gets 14 days free on either plan. You add a card when you choose the plan, and
nothing is charged until the trial ends. Each account gets one trial, so a second workspace pays
from its first day.

## What this month used

**Usage** shows the views and uploads of this calendar month, and the storage you use now, against
your plan. The month runs from the 1st to the 1st in UTC, whatever day your plan renews:

The bars turn orange at 80% and red at 100%. Going over doesn't stop anything: your documents keep
opening. Usage is where you see how close you are, so look in now and then.

## Change your plan

Owners and admins change the plan, update the card and cancel the renewal on **Billing**, where the
invoices are too.

To change the plan, click **Change plan**, pick one, and check the price before you confirm. The
new limits apply right away. An upgrade charges the difference now, and a downgrade leaves a credit
for your next invoices.

Without a plan, the workspace keeps your documents, but they don't open: links, embeds and API keys
stop within a minute, until it has a plan again. Members, Billing and Settings still work.
