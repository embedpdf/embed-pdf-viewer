# Members and roles

Everyone in a workspace works with the same documents, grants and API keys. Invite your team from
**Members**:

  name="members"
  alt="The Members page of Fernquill Studio: its members with their roles, and the Invite someone card"

## Roles

| Role | Can |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Owner** | Everything, including billing. The person who made the workspace |
| **Admin** | Everything an owner can, except change the owner |
| **Member** | Work with documents, folders and grants, and see the API keys |

Only owners and admins invite people, change roles, remove members, make and revoke API keys, and
see **Billing**.

## Invite someone

Type their business email, choose **Admin** or **Member**, and click **Send invite**. They get an
email with a link to join, which works for 48 hours.

Without a CloudPDF account, they make one with that address, then join. A personal email address
can't make an account, so invite them at work.

Until they join, the invite waits in the list, where you can **Resend** or **Cancel** it.

## Change a role, or remove someone

Each member's row has their role, which you can change, and **Remove**. Removing someone takes
their access away at once. Nobody can change the owner, or their own row.

Anyone but the owner can **Leave** a workspace from their own row. They need a new invite to come
back.
