# Passphrase, expiry and questions

Under **More options**, a grant can ask for a passphrase, end on a date, and ask visitors who they
are. Use any of them, or all three.

<Screenshot
  name="grant-more"
  alt="More options: a passphrase, an expiry date, and the questions visitors answer, with Name and Email required"
/>

## A passphrase

Visitors type it before the document opens. Send it apart from the link, in another message or out
loud, so the link alone isn't enough.

CloudPDF keeps only a fingerprint of the passphrase, so nobody can read it back, not even you. To
change it, type a new one; to drop it, tick **Remove the passphrase**. Guessing doesn't work:
after too many wrong tries in a minute, CloudPDF stops answering that network for a while.

In code it's `passphrase`, on the grant and in the viewer:

```html
<embedpdf-viewer grant="grt_…" passphrase="quill-review-2026"></embedpdf-viewer>
```

Leave it out, and the viewer asks for it.

## An expiry date

The grant works until the end of that day, in your time zone, then stops: its link, and every page
that embeds it. The list shows it as **Expired**. Pick a later date to bring it back.

## Questions

Tick **Ask who they are**, and choose what to ask:

| Question | Goes on |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Name** | Everything they add, as its author |
| **Email**, **Company**, **Job title**, **Department** | Their row under [visitors](/docs/platform/grants/visitors) |

Each is **Required**, **Optional**, or **Don't ask**. A new grant starts with Name and Email
required.

Ask **Before it opens**, or, when visitors can change the document, **Before their first change**:
then anyone can read, and only someone who adds something answers first.

Say why you ask under **Why you ask**, and link your **Privacy notice**. Both show with the
questions.

A visitor answers once in their browser, and can't change an answer later. Only their name goes
into the document.

## In code

```ts
await cloudpdf.grants.create({
  docId: proposal.documentId,
  name: 'Client review',
  scope: permissions.comment,
  passphrase: 'quill-review-2026',
  expiresAt: Date.parse('2026-12-31T23:59:59+01:00'),
  visitorIdentity: {
    fields: { displayName: 'required', email: 'required', organization: 'optional' },
    ask: 'on-open',
    purpose: 'We reply to every comment.',
    privacyUrl: 'https://www.fernquill.example/privacy',
  },
});
```

`ask` is `'on-open'` or `'before-first-change'`. `expiresAt` is a time in milliseconds, and `null`
removes it.
