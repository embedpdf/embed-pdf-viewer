# Visitors

A grant's visitors are the people who opened it and left something behind: answers to its
questions, or changes. Open the grant's menu and choose **Visitors**:

<Screenshot
  name="grant-visitors"
  alt="The Visitors dialog of the Client review grant, listing two visitors with their answers"
/>

Each visitor shows their answers, when they were last seen, and their own copy, if the grant gives
them one. Someone who only read the document, and wasn't asked anything, isn't listed: CloudPDF
remembers nothing about them.

**Export CSV** downloads the list, with when each visitor answered.

## Erasing a visitor

**Delete** erases a visitor's answers and their copy, for good. If they come back, they're a new
visitor, and they answer again. Do this when someone asks you to delete what you know about them.

What they added to a shared document stays, with their name on it, since it's part of the
document now.

## How CloudPDF remembers them

A visitor's browser keeps a key that says "this is the same visitor", so they answer once and find
their own copy again. In another browser, or after clearing this one, they're someone new.

## In code

```ts
const { visitors } = await cloudpdf.grants.visitors.list({ grantId });

await cloudpdf.grants.visitors.delete({ grantId, visitorId: visitors[0].visitorId });
```
