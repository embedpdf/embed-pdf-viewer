# Uploading

Upload PDFs on **Documents**: click **Upload** and pick one or more, or drop them on the page. They
go into the folder you're in.

<Screenshot
  name="documents"
  alt="The Documents page of the Fernquill Studio workspace, with its folders and PDFs, and the Upload button"
/>

Each file uploads in turn, and the page says which one it's on. A file that isn't a PDF is skipped,
and the page tells you which.

A new document is **Pending** for a moment while CloudPDF reads it, then it's ready to open and
share. One that can't be read, because it's damaged, say, is marked **Failed**.

## A document's page

Click a document for its page: its size, when it was uploaded, its **Document ID** for your code,
and its [grants](/docs/platform/grants).

<Screenshot
  name="new-grant"
  alt="The page of Brand guidelines.pdf: its size and ID, and a Grants card"
/>

**Open** shows it full screen in the viewer, with everything you can do.

## Rename, move and delete

A document's menu, on its page or in the list, has **Rename**, **Move to…** and **Delete**:

- **Rename** changes the name the dashboard shows. Links and embeds keep working.
- **Move to…** puts it in another folder. Links and embeds keep working.
- **Delete** removes it for good, and its grants with it: every link and embed stops at once.

Select several in the list to move or delete them together.

## The same file twice

Uploading a file you uploaded before makes a second document, with its own grants. That's on
purpose: the two can go to different people. Your server can [reuse the existing
one](/docs/platform/app/uploads#the-same-file-twice) instead.
