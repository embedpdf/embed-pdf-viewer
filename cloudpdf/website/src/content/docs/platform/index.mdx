# Put PDFs in front of people

Upload a PDF to CloudPDF, choose who can open it and what they can do, and it opens in a full PDF
viewer on your website or in your app. CloudPDF keeps the file, draws the pages, and checks every
request against what you allowed.

## A grant or a token

Who opens the document decides how they get in:

| | A grant | A token |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Who opens it | Anyone with the link, or on your website | Your own signed-in users |
| Made in | The dashboard | Your server, with an API key |
| Knows who they are | No: they're visitors | Yes: your user's id and name |
| Works until | You pause or end it | It runs out, after minutes or hours |

Both end the same way: the viewer gets a short-lived pass for one document, with the permissions
you chose, and every page, search and comment is checked against it.

## What people can do

A grant and a token say what people can do in the same words:

| Level | People can |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Read** | See the pages, search and select text, see comments and form values |
| **Comment** | Read, and add comments, highlights and drawings, then change and delete their own |
| **Fill and sign** | Read, and fill in the form, submit it and sign it |
| **Edit** | Everything above on anyone's work, and change pages, content and details |

On top of any level, they can **Download** the PDF, **Print** it, and **Copy text** from it, but
only when you say so.

## What you work with

- **The dashboard**, at [app.cloudpdf.com](https://app.cloudpdf.com): your documents and folders,
  grants, API keys, members and billing.
- **The viewer**: two lines of HTML, or a component for React, Vue, Svelte and Angular. It brings
  the toolbar, search, comments, forms and signatures. Or build your own interface with the
  [headless components](/docs/headless/react).
- **The SDK**, for your server: uploads, tokens and everything in the
  [API reference](/docs/api-reference), in seven languages.
