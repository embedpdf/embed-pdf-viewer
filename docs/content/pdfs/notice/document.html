Notice

CDN maintenance on 4 November

Sent 20 October 2026 · To teams that load EmbedPDF from snippet.embedpdf.com

On 4 November we move snippet.embedpdf.com to a new CDN. Scripts and the PDF engine keep their addresses, so most apps need no change.

Schedule

StepDateTime (UTC)
Every version copied to the new CDN3 NovemberAll day
Traffic switched to the new CDN4 November07:00 to 08:00
Old CDN serves files read-only4 to 18 NovemberAll day
Old CDN switched off18 November09:00

What to check

Apps that load a version range, such as @3, pick up the copy on the new CDN by themselves. Apps that pin one build by its hash should check that the hash still loads once the copy is done.

If your content security policy lists the CDN by its IP addresses rather than its name, add the new addresses from the status page before the switch.

Questions

Reply to this notice, or write to support@embedpdf.com. We post every step on status.embedpdf.com as it happens.
