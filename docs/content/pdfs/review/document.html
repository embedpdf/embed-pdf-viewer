Highlights and notes

Say it
in the margin.

Highlight a phrase, leave a note, answer one. Every mark on this page is an annotation, saved in the PDF.

A good review starts with one clear mark. A highlight says “look here”, a note says why, and both stay in the PDF for the next person who opens it.

Text markup comes in four kinds. A highlight colours the text, an underline stresses it, a strikeout proposes to cut it, and a squiggly line questions it.

A note starts a conversation. Replies collect under it as a thread, and the person who asked can accept or reject what comes back.
