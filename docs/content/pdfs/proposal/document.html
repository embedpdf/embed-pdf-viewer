Proposal

A comments sidebar for the ready-made viewer

Ada Lovelace · 14 October 2026 · Reviewer: Dana Smith In review

Problem

Notes in a PDF are hard to follow. Each one is a small icon on the page with its replies hidden behind it, so a reviewer opens them one by one to read a conversation.

Teams that review contracts and reports tell us the same thing: they move the discussion to email, and the PDF stops being the place where decisions are made.

Proposal

Add a sidebar that lists every comment thread in reading order. A click on a thread scrolls to its mark on the page; replies and review states show in the sidebar, under the comment they answer.

The sidebar replaces the old floating popups entirely, which keeps one place to read and write comments.
