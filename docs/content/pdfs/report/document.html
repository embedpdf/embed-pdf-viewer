What shipped

Forms were the biggest release. Every kind of field now fills in, checks its value and adds up totals, and the form designer turns any page into a form.

Annotations gained comments with replies and review states. Stamps can be made from anything drawn on the page, and text boxes keep their fonts, colours and line breaks in every app our customers open them in.

Under the hood, documents open faster: the first page of a 400-page file now shows in a third of the time it took in June.
