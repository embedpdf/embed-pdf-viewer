What shipped

Forms were the biggest release. Every kind of field now fills in, checks its value and adds up totals, and the form designer turns any page into a form.

Annotations gained comments with replies and review states. Stamps can be made from anything drawn on the page, and text boxes keep their fonts, colours and line breaks in every PDF app.

Under the hood, documents open faster: the first page of a 400-page file now shows in a third of the time it took in June.

What's next

The 3.0 release ships in November, with the headless packages for React, Vue, Svelte and Angular and a new documentation site that shows every feature in each of them.

After the release we start on measurements and on organizing pages, the two features people ask for most in issues and discussions.

Thanks

Thank you to everyone who sent code, documentation and bug reports this quarter, and to the sponsors who make the work possible.
