Support agreement

Between EmbedPDF and Analytical Engines Ltd · Reference SA-2026-031

EmbedPDF gives Analytical Engines Ltd priority support for the EmbedPDF libraries it uses in production, on the terms below.

  1. Scope. Support covers the open source EmbedPDF packages and the PDF engine, in the versions EmbedPDF still maintains. It doesn't cover changes to the customer's own code.
  2. Response times. EmbedPDF answers an issue that stops production within one working day, and any other issue within three working days.
  3. Term. The agreement runs for twelve months from 1 November 2026. It renews each year unless either side ends it in writing with 30 days' notice.
  4. Price. $1,440 a year, invoiced in advance and due within 30 days.
  5. Confidentiality. Both sides keep what they learn about each other's systems to themselves, during the agreement and after it ends.
