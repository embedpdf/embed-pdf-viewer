Section 01

Logo

The logo is the mark, a quill drawn as a leaf, followed by the name. They always appear together, in this order, at the same proportions.

On white, with its clear space marked.
On Mist green, the lightest colour of the palette.

Clear space

Keep a margin around the logo at least as wide as the mark is tall. Nothing else goes inside it: no text, no other logo, no edge of the page.

Size

The logo is at least 24 pixels tall on a screen and 8 millimetres tall in print. Below that the name stops being readable; use the mark on its own instead.

Where it goes

Put the logo on white or on Mist green. The colours, and the contrast each one gives, are on page 3.

  • Don't stretch, rotate or recolour it.
  • Don't put it on a photo or on a busy pattern.
  • Don't set the name in another typeface next to the mark.

Section 02

Colour

Five colours, all from one green. Forest carries the words, Fern green marks what responds to a click, and the light tints sit behind panels and badges.

ColourHexUse it forContrast on white1
Forest#0F3324Headings and body text13.8 : 1
Fern green#1B7A4BLinks, buttons and the logo5.3 : 1
Moss#4C6157Captions, dates and other quiet text6.7 : 1
Sage#86C4A2Illustrations, charts and outlines2.0 : 1
Mist green#E3F2EABackgrounds behind panels and badges1.2 : 1

Text on colour

Words are Forest or Moss. Sage and Mist green are too light to carry text on white; put Forest text on them instead, which reads at 6.9 : 1 on Sage and 11.9 : 1 on Mist green.

Fern green is kept for links and buttons, so a reader learns that green means something happens. Its sizes are with the type, on page 4.

1 The ratio between a colour and white, worked out as the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines define it: www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22.

Section 03

Type

Three typefaces, each with one job. All three are open source, so anyone who makes something for Fernquill can use them too.

ManropeTitles and headings Every document in one place
InterParagraphs, tables and captions Share, review and sign documents with your clients, all in one place.
JetBrains MonoCode, colours and figures Fern green #1B7A4B · 5.3 : 1

Sizes

Titles are 32 pixels, headings 16 and paragraphs 13.5, with a line height of 1.65. A link in Fern green uses the size of the text around it and no underline.
