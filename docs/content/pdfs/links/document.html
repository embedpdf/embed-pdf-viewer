Section 01

Logo

The logo is the mark, three overlapping squares, followed by the name. They always appear together, in this order, at the same proportions.

On white, with its clear space marked.
On Mist blue, the lightest colour of the palette.

Clear space

Keep a margin around the logo at least as wide as the mark is tall. Nothing else goes inside it: no text, no other logo, no edge of the page.

Size

The logo is at least 24 pixels tall on a screen and 8 millimetres tall in print. Below that the name stops being readable; use the mark on its own instead.

Where it goes

Put the logo on white or on Mist blue. The colours, and the contrast each one gives, are on page 3.

  • Don't stretch, rotate or recolour it.
  • Don't put it on a photo or on a busy pattern.
  • Don't set the name in another typeface next to the mark.

Section 02

Colour

Five colours, all from one blue. Deep navy carries the words, Brand blue marks what responds to a click, and the light tints sit behind panels and badges.

ColourHexUse it forContrast on white1
Deep navy#0A1A4DHeadings and body text16.6 : 1
Brand blue#1677FFLinks, buttons and the logo4.1 : 1
Slate#515B7ECaptions, dates and other quiet text6.7 : 1
Sky blue#7DB6FFIllustrations, charts and outlines2.1 : 1
Mist blue#E6F0FFBackgrounds behind panels and badges1.1 : 1

Text on colour

Words are Deep navy or Slate. Sky blue and Mist blue are too light to carry text on white; put Deep navy text on them instead, which reads at 7.9 : 1 on Sky blue and 14.4 : 1 on Mist blue.

Brand blue is kept for links and buttons, so a reader learns that blue means something happens. Its sizes are with the type, on page 4.

1 The ratio between a colour and white, worked out as the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines define it: www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22.

Section 03

Type

Three typefaces, each with one job. All three are open source, so anyone who builds with EmbedPDF can use them too.

ManropeTitles and headings Documents belong on the web
InterParagraphs, tables and captions Open, read and sign a PDF in the browser, without sending it anywhere.
JetBrains MonoCode, colours and figures npm install @embedpdf/react

Sizes

Titles are 32 pixels, headings 16 and paragraphs 13.5, with a line height of 1.65. A link in Brand blue uses the size of the text around it and no underline.
