# Outline

A document's outline is its table of contents: titled items, nested, each going to a place in the
document. Acrobat calls the items bookmarks. Register `outlinePlugin()` to show it in your UI:

<Snippet name="documents/outline-list" />

What `useOutlineState()` gives you updates by itself: after your own changes, and on a shared
document, after someone else's. Nothing is read when a document opens: the outline loads the first
time something asks for it, so a document nobody shows the outline of costs nothing.

## Show the outline

`rows` are the rows a tree shows: every item whose parents are all expanded, depth first. Each row
has the `item`, its `depth` (0 at the top level), `hasChildren`, and whether it's `expanded`.
`items` is every item, folded or not, when you'd rather build the tree yourself.

`toggle(ref)` folds or unfolds an item; `expand(ref)`, `collapse(ref)`, `expandAll()` and
`collapseAll()` do one of them. The items the file opens start expanded, and from then on what's
expanded is the reader's: it doesn't change the document.

An item has its `title`, its `color` (`null` when the file sets none, so draw your own text
colour), and whether it's `bold` or `italic`. `status` says whether the outline hasn't been asked
for yet (`'idle'`), is loading, is there (`'ready'`), or couldn't be read. `incomplete` is `true`
when part of the outline couldn't be read, such as a loop in a damaged file; what could be read is
listed.

## Follow an item

`activate(ref)` goes where the item goes, as a click does: a page moves the view, a website opens,
and an item that runs a script runs it, through the [actions plugin](/docs/headless/documents/actions)
when you've registered it. It returns what happened at once, so a website can still open from the
reader's click.

`current` is the item for where the reader is: the last item, in reading order, that goes to a place
at or above the top of the view. Mark its row, and the reader always sees where they are in the
outline:

```ts
const isCurrent = row.item.ref.objectNumber === current?.objectNumber;
```

## Change the outline

`create()` adds an item. Leave out where it goes and it goes to the place in view; leave out its
title and it takes the selected text, or "Untitled" in the viewer's language. It shows at once, and
`select: true` selects it and unfolds the items it sits under, so it's ready to rename:

```ts
await outline.create({ parent: chapter.ref, position: 'end' }, { select: true });
```

`update(ref, changes)` changes the fields you pass: a `title`, a `target`, a `color`, `bold`,
`italic`, or `open` (whether it's expanded when the document opens). `null` removes a target or a
colour. `move(refs, parent, position)` moves items, with the items under them, to their new place,
and `delete(refs)` deletes items with the items under them.

Each of these shows at once, is one step to undo, and resolves once the engine has it. A place is
a neighbour, not a number: `'start'`, `'end'`, `{ before: ref }` or `{ after: ref }` among the
parent's children.

## Select items

`select(refs)` makes items the selection, and `selected` lists them. The selection is the
reader's, like what's expanded: show it, and act on it with your own buttons.

```ts
outline.select([row.item.ref]);
await outline.delete(selected);
```

## Methods

Everything `useOutline()` gives you.

| Method                              | What it does                                                                                                                     |
| :---------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `get(ref)`                          | One item, or `null`.                                                                                                             |
| `getChildren(parent)`               | An item's children in order, or the top level's for `null`.                                                                      |
| `isExpanded(ref)`                   | Whether an item's children show. [Show the outline](#show-the-outline)                                                           |
| `toggle(ref)`                       | Folds an expanded item, unfolds a folded one.                                                                                    |
| `expand(ref)`, `collapse(ref)`      | Unfolds or folds one item.                                                                                                       |
| `expandAll()`, `collapseAll()`      | Unfolds or folds every item.                                                                                                     |
| `reveal(ref)`                       | Unfolds every item `ref` sits under, so its row shows.                                                                           |
| `select(refs)`                      | Makes these items the selection; `[]` clears it. [Select items](#select-items)                                                   |
| `activate(ref)`                     | Goes where the item goes, as a click does, and returns what happened. [Follow an item](#follow-an-item)                          |
| `getCurrentViewTarget()`            | Where the view is, as a target: the page at its top and the spot at its top-left. What a new item goes to.                       |
| `create(item, options)`             | Adds an item; `select: true` selects it. Resolves `{ item }`. [Change the outline](#change-the-outline)                          |
| `update(ref, changes)`              | Changes the fields you pass; `null` removes a target or a colour. Resolves `{ item }`.                                           |
| `move(refs, parent, position)`      | Moves items, with the items under them, to their new place.                                                                      |
| `delete(refs)`                      | Deletes items with the items under them, as one change.                                                                          |
| `ensureLoaded()`                    | Reads the outline from the file, unless it's loaded or loading. Resolves once it's there.                                        |
| `refresh()`                         | Reads the outline again. Rarely needed: it stays up to date by itself.                                                           |

Every method that returns a promise also takes a `signal` in its options, to cancel it.

## State

<StateIntro hook="useOutlineState()" />

| Field        | What it is                                                                                           | Read once           |
| :----------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------ |
| `items`      | Every item, depth first, with your changes shown before the engine answers                           | `list()`            |
| `rows`       | The rows a tree shows: `item`, `depth`, `hasChildren`, `expanded`                                    | `listRows()`        |
| `current`    | The item for where the reader is, or `null`                                                          | `getCurrentItem()`  |
| `selected`   | The selected items, in the order they were selected                                                  | `getSelected()`     |
| `status`     | `'idle'` until something asks for it, then `'loading'`, `'ready'`, `'forbidden'` or `'error'`        | `getStatus()`       |
| `incomplete` | Part of the outline couldn't be read                                                                 | `isIncomplete()`    |
| `canEdit`    | Whether changing the outline is allowed                                                              | `canEdit()`         |

## Events

<Snippet name="documents/outline-changed" />

| Event        | Fires when                                                                                             | Carries                    |
| :----------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------- |
| `onChanged`  | Items were created, updated, moved, deleted or restored, by you or by someone else                     | `type`, `items`, `origin`  |
| `onResynced` | The outline was read from the file: the first time it's asked for, after `refresh()`, or to catch up after a lost connection | `outline` |

`type` is `'created'`, `'updated'`, `'moved'`, `'deleted'` or `'restored'`, and `items` names the
items it touched; for a delete, the item first, then the items under it.

## Permissions

| Permission           | What it allows                                 | Check                |
| :------------------- | :--------------------------------------------- | :------------------- |
| `doc.open`           | Reading the outline                            | none: it opened      |
| `doc.outline.modify` | Adding, changing, moving and deleting items    | `outline.canEdit()`  |

## Good to know

### Expanded, and open

What's expanded is the reader's view, not the document: folding an item doesn't change the file.
An item's `open` is the file's own setting, whether its children show when the document opens, and
the outline starts from it. Change it with `update(ref, { open })`.

### Pages that are gone

Deleting a page doesn't delete the items that go to it: their target reads `unsupported`, and
`activate()` reports it rather than going anywhere. See
[Outline](/docs/engine/documents/outline#pages-that-are-gone).

### The ready-made viewer

The viewer's Outline panel is built on this plugin. See
[Panels](/docs/viewer/customize/panels).
