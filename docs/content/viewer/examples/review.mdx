# Contract review

For a legal or sales team reviewing a document someone sent them. The viewer gets an app bar of its
own with the document's name, the review status and the verdict; the review tools sit on a rail,
the notes stay docked on the right, and a checklist of its own opens beside the rail. Its colors,
font and words are the review's.

<Example name="viewer/examples/review" size="wide" />

> **Try it:** read the thread on Dana Smith's note next to the title in **Review notes**, then tick
> the items in the **Checklist** at the bottom of the rail. **Approve** turns the status next to the
> document's name green; **Request changes** turns it red and picks the note tool, so your next
> click on the page leaves a note.

## What it uses

- [Bars of its own](../customize/layout#several-toolbars): the review bar on top and the tools
  rail, in place of the main toolbar and the mode bars, which it
  [removes](../customize/layout#remove-buttons).
- [Its own components](../customize/components) in the review bar: the document's name and its
  status.
- [Its own commands](../customize/commands), with [their own icons](../customize/commands#icons)
  and a shortcut: **Approve** and **Request changes**, with their labels while there's
  [room](../customize/layout#when-theres-no-room).
- [A panel of its own](../customize/panels#your-own-panel), the checklist, with search and style
  [moved](../customize/panels) to its side, so the right side stays the notes'.
- [How the panels open](../customize/panels#how-a-panel-opens): docked at medium widths too, and the
  notes 340 px wide.
- [A floating bar](../customize/layout#a-floating-toolbar): the zoom field beside the page number.
- [Small screens](../customize/small-screens): on a phone the rail moves to the bottom and the
  document's name leaves the bar.
- [The theme's tokens](../customize/theming#every-color), with a dark canvas and its own `font`.
- [Its own words](../customize/translations#change-a-word) for the tools, and a
  [`titleKey`](../customize/translations#translate-your-own-buttons) that renames Comments to
  Review notes.
- [Features turned off](../features/turning-off) that a review doesn't need.
- [The tools' defaults](../features/annotations#the-tools): red for deletions, the review's color
  for suggestions.
- [The viewer in code](../code/viewer#react-to-what-happens): once the document opens, the notes
  open and the name comes from the [metadata plugin](../code/plugins#every-plugin).
