# A front-desk kiosk

For a tablet on a counter: someone fills in an application and hands it back. The form is all
there is, under a header with the desk's name and how many required answers are in. Three big
buttons at the bottom take them to the next empty box, clear the form for the next person, and
check it: **Done** lists what's still missing, or locks the finished form and says thank you.

<Example name="viewer/examples/kiosk" size="wide" />

> **Try it:** tap **Next field** to go to Full name and fill it in, then tap **Done**: the sheet
> lists Email and the membership terms, and a tap on one takes you there. Answer both, tap **Done**
> again, then **Clear** for the next person.

## What it uses

- [A whole layout](../customize/layout#your-whole-layout): one bar at the bottom, and a panel.
- [Its own commands](../customize/commands), with [their own icons](../customize/commands#icons):
  **Next field**, **Clear** and **Done**, with Clear
  [greyed out](../customize/commands#enabled-active-and-visible) while the form is empty.
- [Its own component](../customize/components#use-the-viewers-commands-and-state) in that bar,
  which draws the three commands as big buttons, and fits a phone through
  [where it sits](../customize/components#know-where-it-sits).
- [The header region](../customize/components#above-below-and-when-nothing-is-open), with the
  desk's name, a line that says what just happened, and the count of required answers.
- [A panel of its own](../customize/panels#your-own-panel) that opens as a
  [sheet](../customize/panels#how-a-panel-opens) at every size, opened and closed
  [from code](../customize/panels#open-a-panel-from-code).
- [The theme's tokens](../customize/theming#every-color) for warm paper, round corners and a rounded
  `font`, which the kiosk's own parts [use too](../customize/theming#in-your-own-components).
- [Read-only, except filling in the form](../features/turning-off#read-only), and
  [read-only](../setup#change-the-config-later) once the form is done, until Clear.
- [Features turned off](../features/turning-off): opening, downloading and printing files, search,
  and selecting text.
- [Plugin settings](../code/plugins#settings): the pages fit the width, and the fields have a tint
  of their own, as [Form settings](../features/forms-and-signatures#form-settings) shows.
- [The form plugin from code](../features/forms-and-signatures#read-and-fill-in-values-from-code):
  the empty fields, [the required ones](/docs/headless/forms#check-required-fields) and
  [a reset](/docs/headless/forms#reset-the-form), with the Stage's
  [`reveal()`](/docs/headless/viewing/stage#jump-to-an-exact-spot) to go to a field.
- [`watch()`](../code/viewer#follow-a-value) on the form, from when
  [the document opens](../code/viewer#react-to-what-happens), for the header and the sheet.
