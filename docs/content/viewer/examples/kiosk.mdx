# A front-desk kiosk

For a tablet on a counter: someone fills in an application and hands it back. The form is all
there is, under a header with the desk's name, the question being answered and how many required
answers are in. Three buttons at the bottom, each a finger's size, put the cursor in the next empty
box, clear the form for the next person, and check it: **Done** lists what's still missing, or
locks the finished form and says thank you.

<Example name="viewer/examples/kiosk" size="page" />

> **Try it:** tap **Next field**: the cursor goes into Full name, and the header names it. Fill it
> in, then tap **Done**: the sheet lists Email and the membership terms, and a tap on one puts the
> cursor in it. Answer both, tap **Done** again, then **Clear** for the next person.

## What it uses

- [A whole layout](../customize/layout#your-whole-layout): one bar at the bottom, and a panel.
- [Its own commands](../customize/commands), with [their own icons](../customize/commands#icons):
  **Next field**, **Clear** and **Done**, with Clear
  [greyed out](../customize/commands#enabled-active-and-visible) while the form is empty. They
  read and change the app's state, whether the form is done and the header's line, through
  [`appState`](../customize/commands#your-apps-state).
- The bar shows them as the viewer's buttons with
  [their labels](../customize/layout#when-theres-no-room), **Done** as its
  [primary button](../customize/layout#a-primary-button), made big outlined pills through
  [their parts](../customize/theming#restyle-a-part), smaller on a phone.
- [`touch: true`](../customize/small-screens#touch): every button and menu row is a finger's size,
  even with a mouse plugged in.
- [The header region](../customize/components#above-below-and-when-nothing-is-open), with the
  desk's name, a line that names the question being answered or says what just happened, and the
  count of required answers.
- [A panel of its own](../customize/panels#your-own-panel) that opens as a
  [sheet](../customize/panels#how-a-panel-opens) at every size, opened and closed
  [from code](../customize/panels#open-a-panel-from-code).
- [The theme's tokens](../customize/theming#every-color) for warm paper, round corners and a rounded
  `font`, which the kiosk's own parts [use too](../customize/theming#in-your-own-components).
- [Read-only, except filling in the form](../features/turning-off#read-only), and
  [read-only](../setup#change-the-config-later) once the form is done, until Clear.
- [Features turned off](../features/turning-off): opening, downloading and printing files, search,
  and selecting text.
- [Plugin settings](../code/plugins#settings): the pages fit the width, and the fields have a tint
  of their own, as [Form settings](../features/forms-and-signatures#form-settings) shows.
- [The form plugin from code](../features/forms-and-signatures#read-and-fill-in-values-from-code):
  [`focusNext()`](../features/forms-and-signatures#take-people-to-a-field) to the next empty field,
  `focus()` to one the sheet lists, `onFocusChanged` for the header's line (see
  [Move between fields](/docs/headless/forms#move-between-fields)),
  [the required fields](/docs/headless/forms#check-required-fields) and
  [a reset](/docs/headless/forms#reset-the-form).
- [`watch()`](../code/viewer#follow-a-value) on the form from `onReady`, which waits for the
  document, for the header's count and the sheet; the cursor's events
  [start from the document](../code/viewer#plugins-come-with-the-document).
