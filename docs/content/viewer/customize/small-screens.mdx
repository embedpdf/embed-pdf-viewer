# Small screens

The viewer adapts to its own width, not the window's. On a phone, or in a narrow column of a wide
screen, it gets a phone's layout: panels and menus slide up from the bottom, and buttons that don't
fit move into the "More" menu. You don't set anything for this.

To change what happens at a size, use the second argument of the layout function:

```js
{
  layout: (layout, env) =>
    env.size === 'compact' ? layout.place('main', 'bottom') : layout,
}
```

On a phone, the main toolbar now sits at the bottom, under the thumb. The function runs again
whenever the size changes, so turning the phone or resizing the window switches layouts as it
happens.

## The three sizes

| `env.size` | The viewer's width | What the default does |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| `'compact'` | Under 600 px | Panels and menus are sheets from the bottom. The mode tabs become one dropdown. The page number hides while you scroll. |
| `'medium'` | 600 to 1023 px | Panels open over the pages instead of pushing them aside. |
| `'expanded'` | 1024 px and wider | Panels dock beside the pages. |

At every size, a toolbar keeps what fits and moves the rest into its "More" menu, least important
first (see [Layout](layout#when-theres-no-room)).

## What `env` tells you

| Field | What it is |
| :-- | :-- |
| `size` | `'compact'`, `'medium'` or `'expanded'` |
| `width` | The viewer's width in px, for a size of your own: `env.width < 480` |
| `touch` | Whether the main way of pointing is a finger |
| `dir` | `'ltr'` or `'rtl'`, from the user's language |

## Sheets

A panel or a menu on a phone is a sheet: it slides up from the bottom of the viewer, over the
pages, with a handle at the top.

- Drag the handle to make it taller or shorter, or down to close it.
- A tap on the pages above it closes it.
- A sheet that holds a list, such as the thumbnails or the search results, closes when you pick
  something, so you see where it took you.

To make a panel a sheet at other sizes too, or never, set its `present`: see
[Panels](panels#how-a-panel-opens).

## Touch

When `env.touch` is true, every button and menu row is at least 44 px tall, and the viewer listens
for touch gestures: pinch to zoom, a long press to select a word, the handles to stretch the
selection.

## The notch and the home bar

When the viewer reaches the edge of the screen, as it does in full screen or as the whole page of
a phone app, its bars and sheets keep clear of the notch, the rounded corners and the home bar.

## Good to know

### Why the viewer's width, not the window's

A viewer in a sidebar or a dashboard card is small even on a big screen, and the layout that works
there is the phone's. Measuring the viewer itself gives every embed the right layout without media
queries.

### Change the sizes

To switch at other widths, decide on `env.width` yourself:

```js
{
  layout: (layout, env) =>
    env.width < 720 ? layout.place('main', 'bottom') : layout,
}
```
